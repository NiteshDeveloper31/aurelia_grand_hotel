25 YEARS OF EXCELLENCE

Our Heritage & Hospitality Story

Discover the story behind AURELIA Grand Hotel, established in 1999 as a premier destination for luxury Indian hospitality.

Atithi Devo Bhava Philosophy

Founded by Shri Vikramaditya Singhania in 1999, AURELIA was built on the sacred Indian principle of 'Guest is God'. Over 25+ years, we have hosted over 10,000+ happy guests, royal weddings, and corporate summits.

Our hotel features 50+ luxury rooms, multi-cuisine fine dining, temperature-controlled swimming pool, Ayurveda spa, and grand pillarless wedding banquets.

"Hospitality is not merely providing a room; it is crafting warm, unforgettable memories for every guest who enters our doors."

— Vikramaditya Singhania, Founder
