25 YEARS OF HOSPITALITY EXCELLENCE

Our Heritage & Hospitality Story

Established in 1999 in Rajasthan, The Haveli Royale is built on the sacred Indian principle of 'Atithi Devo Bhava' — Guest is God.

FOUNDER'S VISION

A Legacy Built on Royal Indian Hospitality

Founded by Shri Vikramaditya Singhania in 1999, The Haveli Royale was created to blend Rajasthan's majestic palace architectural grandeur with modern 5-star comfort and world-class luxury standards.

Over 25+ years, we have welcomed over 10,000+ happy guests from across India and worldwide, hosting royal weddings, corporate summits, state banquets, and unforgettable family celebrations.

"Hospitality is not merely providing a room; it is crafting warm, unforgettable memories for every guest who enters our doors."

— Shri Vikramaditya Singhania, Founder

22+ Years of Royal Indian Culinary Heritage
